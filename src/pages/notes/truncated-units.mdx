## Abstract

Let $p$ be a prime and $n\geq 1$. We give a proof of the natural equivalence
between the Postnikov slices of a $p$-local $E_\infty$-ring spectrum and its
spectrum of units in degrees $n\leq i<pn$. The proof uses the Goodwillie
tower of the reduced suspension spectrum of an infinite loop space. Tate
vanishing splits the first $p-1$ stages and supplies a section of the
truncated stabilization counit. Testing this section on truncated sphere
spectra identifies its effect on homotopy groups, allowing a based space
equivalence to be replaced by a spectrum equivalence in the stated range.
We also record the factorial-invertibility variant and identify the induced
comparison on finite complexes with the truncated logarithm. Counterexamples
for spectra of units show that the upper endpoint cannot be included uniformly.
