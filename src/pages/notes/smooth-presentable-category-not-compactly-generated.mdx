## Abstract

We construct a dualizable presentable stable $\mathbb{Q}$-linear category
whose coevaluation is an internal left adjoint, but which is not compactly
generated. The category is the kernel of extension of scalars along a
quotient of a skew Laurent algebra. Its defining ideal is flat and
idempotent, and is perfect as a bimodule. A finite resolution obtained
from the action on the fractional powers proves the bimodule perfectness.
The classical division ring of fractions is a nonzero object of the kernel
orthogonal to every compact object. This gives a counterexample to
Ramzi's Conjecture 0.6.
