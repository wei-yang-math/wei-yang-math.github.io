## Abstract

For every field $k$ of characteristic zero, we construct a local algebraic
$k$-algebra $A$ with $L_{A/k}\simeq0$, $A_{\mathrm{red}}\cong k$, and a
nonzero square-zero element. In particular, $A$ is not ind-étale over $k$.
The construction is a sequential filtered colimit of finite local complete
intersections: the transition maps annihilate cotangent homology, while
an explicit exponent bound preserves a chosen nilpotent.
It answers Bhatt's existence question and the non-reduced formulation of
Mondal and Mukhopadhyay affirmatively. For $k=\mathbb{Q}$, it also realizes
the case $A_{\mathrm{red}}\cong\mathbb{Q}$ singled out in Antieau's Question 6.
