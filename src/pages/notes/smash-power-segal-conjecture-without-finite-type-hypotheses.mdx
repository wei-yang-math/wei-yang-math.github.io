## Abstract

Let $G\neq 1$ be a finite $p$-group and let $B$ be a bounded-below spectrum.
We prove that the proper Tate diagonal

$$
\Delta_G(B)\colon B\longrightarrow
\bigl(B^{\wedge G}\bigr)^{\tau G}
$$

is the stable $p$-completion map. Equivalently, the comparison from the
categorical fixed points of the Hill-Hopkins-Ravenel norm $N_e^G B$ to its
homotopy fixed points is a mod-$p$ equivalence. The published theorem of
Bergsaker-Rognes proves this under a degreewise finite mod-$p$ homology
hypothesis. The new argument uses their theorem only for finite-type
Eilenberg-Mac Lane input.

The finite-type hypothesis is removed by studying the representation-sphere
obstruction

$$
Q_G(B)=F_G(S^{\infty\bar\rho_G},N_e^G B)^G.
$$

Finite representation stages, a uniform pro-zero argument, exactness, and a
Postnikov-continuity statement specific to norm inputs imply that
$Q_G(B)/p\simeq0$ for every bounded-below $B$. Isotropy separation and
induction on $|G|$ then finish the argument. An appendix records why the
stronger assertion that proper Tate constructions commute with arbitrary
bounded-below Postnikov towers is false.
