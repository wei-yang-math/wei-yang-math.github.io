## Abstract

We construct a finite Smith-Toda complex $V(4)$ at every prime $p\geq 11$.
Its Brown-Peterson homology is the canonical quotient comodule
$BP_*/(p,v_1,v_2,v_3,v_4)$, and it has $32$ cells.
The construction uses a sphere map realizing $v_4$ in $V(3)$.
We prove that this map exists by calculating the possible Adams-Novikov
differentials and applying a relation in the homotopy groups of spheres
to the final target. The prime threshold is sharp by Nave's nonexistence
theorem at $p=7$.
