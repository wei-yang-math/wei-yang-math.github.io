## Abstract

At the prime $2$, we construct a connective $\mathbb{E}_\infty$-ring $C$ such
that

$$
K(1) \wedge C \simeq 0
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
H\mathbb{Q} \wedge C^{tC_2} \not\simeq 0.
$$

Thus $K(1)$-acyclicity of a connective $\mathbb{E}_\infty$-ring does not
imply rational acyclicity of its $C_2$-Tate construction. The example is the
versal $ku_{(2)}$-algebra obtained by adjoining a degree-$4$ class $b$ and a
nullhomotopy of $2b-u^2$, where $u$ is the Bott class.

The proof uses an affine weight filtration whose layers are extended powers of
the mod-$2$ Moore spectrum. McClure's finite-coefficient Bockstein calculation,
followed by a Milnor inverse-limit argument, bounds the denominators detected
by completed periodic $K$-theory. A Laurent-series model for the Tate
constructions of the finite Postnikov sections then gives, for every $m\geq0$,

$$
\operatorname{ord}\!\left(1\in
\pi_0\bigl((\tau_{\leq 4m}C)^{tC_2}\bigr)\right)
\geq 2^{s_2(m)+1}.
$$

Here $s_2(m)$ is the binary digit sum of $m$. These orders are unbounded,
which implies that $C^{tC_2}$ is not rationally acyclic.
