## Abstract

Fix a prime $p$ and an integer $n\geq0$. We prove that every finite
$G$-Galois extension $R\to S$ of $T(n)$-local commutative ring
spectra satisfies

$$
L_{T(n+1)}K(R)\simeq \bigl(L_{T(n+1)}K(S)\bigr)^{hG}.
$$

For $n\geq1$, we establish prime-to-$p$ cyclic descent by a
comparison of crossed-product Hochschild sectors, realized by a
morphism of cyclotomic spectra. For $n=0$, we prove that even
invertible modules of order prime to $p$ have class $1$ in the zeroth homotopy group of
height-one localized $K$-theory of arbitrary rational rings.
This follows from invariance under affine torsors for even
invertible modules and a finite cell argument. The
family-completeness theorem of Clausen-Mathew-Naumann-Noel
then gives descent for every finite group in both cases.
