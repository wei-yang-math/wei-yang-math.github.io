## Abstract

For every compact Lie group $G$, we prove that the canonical homomorphism
$\rho_G:V(G)\to V'(G)$ from the Grothendieck group of homotopy
representations to that of generalized homotopy representations is an
isomorphism. This settles Conjecture 4.5 of Fausk, Lewis, and May and
identifies $V(G)$ with the Picard group $\operatorname{Pic}(\mathrm{Sp}^G)$
of the symmetric monoidal $\infty$-category of genuine $G$-spectra.
Together with earlier work of Fausk-Lewis-May and Bauer, this yields a
canonical short exact sequence

$$
0\longrightarrow\operatorname{Pic}(A(G))\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Pic}(\mathrm{Sp}^G)\longrightarrow D(G)\longrightarrow 0
$$

for every compact Lie group $G$, where $A(G)$ is the Burnside ring and
$D(G)$ is Bauer's group of dimension functions. At the unstable level,
we show that for every generalized homotopy representation $Y$ of $G$
there exist a finite-dimensional real $G$-representation $V$ and a
homotopy representation $X$ equivalent to $Y\wedge S^V$ in
$\mathcal{S}_*^G$.
