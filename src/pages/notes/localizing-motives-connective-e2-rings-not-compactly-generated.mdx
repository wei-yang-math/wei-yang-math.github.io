## Abstract

We prove that Efimov's category of finitary localizing motives over any nonzero
connective $\mathbb{E}_2$-ring spectrum is not compactly generated; more generally,
this holds over every $\mathbb{E}_2$-ring that admits an $\mathbb{E}_2$-map to a field.
Specifically, the motive of the free algebra on one generator in degree $-2$
does not lie in the localizing subcategory generated by the compact motives,
removing the rationality hypothesis in a recent theorem of Ramzi. Over a
perfect field of positive characteristic $p$, we construct an explicit nonzero
motive that is right orthogonal to all compact motives. It is built from the
fiber of the mod-$p$ cyclotomic trace on the square-zero extension
$k\oplus\Sigma k$: orthogonality to compact motives follows from a
Dundas-Goodwillie-McCarthy theorem with compact motivic coefficients, while
nontriviality is detected on the test algebra via Artin-Schreier classes in
topological cyclic homology.
