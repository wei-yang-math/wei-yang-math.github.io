## Abstract

For a map $R\to S$ of $\mathbb{E}_\infty$-rings, its $n$th finite Amitsur
stage is

$$
\operatorname{Tot}^n\bigl(S^{\otimes_R(\bullet+1)}\bigr).
$$

Antieau and Stefanich asked five questions about multiplicative retractions
from these finite stages. We answer all five.

For every prime $p$ and every $k\geq 2$, the restriction map

$$
C^*(BC_{p^k};\mathbb{F}_p)\longrightarrow C^*(BC_p;\mathbb{F}_p)
$$

is descendable but not $\mathbb{E}_\infty$-descendable, and hence is not
$\mathrm{DAlg}$-descendable; for $k=1$ the map is the identity. Thus ordinary
and $\mathbb{E}_\infty$-descendability differ even for cochains on finite
cyclic $p$-groups.

Complexification $KO\to KU$ is $\mathbb{E}_\infty$-descendable: the unit

$$
KO\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Tot}^5\bigl(KU^{\otimes_{KO}(\bullet+1)}\bigr)
$$

admits a retraction in $\mathrm{CAlg}_{KO}$. The units at stages $0$, $1$,
and $2$ do not split in $\mathrm{CAlg}_{KO}$, and the unit at stage $3$ does
not split even as an $\mathbb{E}_1$-$KO$-algebra map. Thus the least
$\mathbb{E}_\infty$-splitting stage is either $4$ or $5$.

Let $A$ be a Noetherian $\mathbb{F}_p$-algebra of finite Krull dimension, put
$d_A=\max\{1,\dim A\}$, and let $A\to B$ be a faithfully flat map of discrete
$\mathbb{F}_p$-algebras. Without any finite-type hypothesis on $B$, the unit
admits a retraction in derived commutative $A$-algebras

$$
\operatorname{Tot}^{d_A+1}\bigl(B^{\otimes_A(\bullet+1)}\bigr)
\longrightarrow A;
$$

specializing to $\mathbb{F}_p[x]\to\mathbb{F}_p[x^{1/p^\infty}]$ gives a
retraction at stage $2$. The proof uses the first two natural layers of a free
derived symmetric algebra, a two-term induced resolution over the Frobenius
skew-polynomial ring $A[F]$, and integral $\mathrm{DAlg}$ flattening.
