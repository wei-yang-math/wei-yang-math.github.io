## Abstract

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $p>0$. We prove that the canonical
map $\mathrm{MGL}/(a_1,a_2,\ldots)\to H\mathbb{Z}$, where the $a_i$ are homogeneous
polynomial generators of the Lazard ring, is an equivalence without
inverting $p$. We also obtain the corresponding equivalence over
$\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb{Z}_p$ for Spitzweck's motivic cohomology spectrum. After
$p$-localization, we show in each positive degree that the image of
weight-zero cobordism homotopy in the Lazard quotient has bounded
exponent and is $p$-divisible, hence vanishes. The exponent bound combines
integral Riemann-Roch with a Hilbert scheme approximation; divisibility
follows by linearizing a geometric power congruence arising from a
deformation of $C_p$ to $\alpha_p$. A filtration by Lazard weight and
the Geisser-Levine theorem then yield the comparison.
