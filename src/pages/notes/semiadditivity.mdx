## Abstract

For every prime $p$, we construct an object in the category of $p$-typical
1-commutative monoids in $p$-local spectra whose underlying spectrum is the
$p$-complete sphere and on which the cardinality of $BC_p$ induces the zero
map on underlying spectra. The construction assigns to a finite groupoid
the group completion of its finite covers, with transfers that retain only
components having trivial relative stabilizer. The Segal conjecture supplies
the Segal condition after $p$-completion. Lee's vanishing theorem then implies
that the norm of the constant $B^2C_p$-diagram on this object is not an
equivalence. This gives a counterexample to the assertion that every
presentable stable 1-semiadditive category is infinitely semiadditive.
We also prove that the assertion holds for compactly generated $p$-local
categories, using a finite-skeleton criterion for bounded stable chromatic
height, and that a compact tensor unit forces rationality.
