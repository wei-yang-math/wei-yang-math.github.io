## Abstract

Let $X$ be a Noetherian quasi-excellent scheme and let $p$ be a prime such
that $X[1/p]$ is regular. For every integer $N$, one power of $p$ annihilates
its polynomial Nil $K$-groups in every degree at most $N$ and every finite
number of variables. For $X=\operatorname{Spec} A$, the exponent can also
be chosen uniformly over all ind-smooth $A$-algebras. The dependence on the
degree bound is necessary even for reduced excellent curves. The proof combines
cdh descent and derived Hochschild homology. Products of presheaves pass
from finite-dimensional local rings to open neighbourhoods. The corresponding
assertion for all Noetherian schemes fails already for one-dimensional
domains in degree zero. We give explicit counterexamples in characteristic
$p$ and in mixed characteristic; the latter are local and flat over
$\mathbb{Z}_{(p)}$ with nonempty regular generic fibre.
