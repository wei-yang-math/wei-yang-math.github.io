## Abstract

The Tate Frobenius of the $K(n)$-local sphere compares the chromatic
overlap $L_{n-1}L_{K(n)}\mathbb{S}$ with the Tate construction
$(L_{K(n)}\mathbb{S})^{t\Sigma_p}$ for the trivial action. We show that this
comparison is a rational equivalence, that it has a retraction after
$K(t)$-localization for every $1\leq t<n$, and that it is an equivalence
for $n\leq 2$. The retraction comes from the trace of a finite etale
cover of degree prime to $p$, normalized by its value on the unit after
descent. At $p=2$ and $n=3$ the comparison is not an equivalence, even after
$K(1)$-localization and reduction modulo $2$. The obstruction is an
Artin-Schreier class attached to lifts modulo $4$ of lines in the
etale Tate module, and it is detected by a transfer.
