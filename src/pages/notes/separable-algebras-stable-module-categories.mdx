## Abstract

Let $k$ be a separably closed field of characteristic $p>0$.
We prove that every separable commutative algebra in the stable module
category of a finite $p$-group is the function algebra of a finite
$p$-group set. After free orbits are removed, the function-algebra
construction is an equivalence of categories. For general finite groups,
we give a negative answer to Balmer's question: in characteristic three,
a $28$-dimensional orthogonal summand of the permutation module on
three-element subsets of a seven-element set yields a separable algebra
$k\oplus L$ in $\operatorname{stmod}(k\mathfrak{A}_7)$ whose underlying stable object is not
a permutation module. The counterexample is proved by explicit module
calculations. The classification uses a relative norm on invariant
vectors, separable algebras over torus Tate cochains, and descent through
normal maximal subgroups. In characteristic two, power operations force
a rational point in the support; a commutative residue algebra at that
point supplies the local freeness needed for the torus classification.
