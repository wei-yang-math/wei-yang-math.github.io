## Abstract

Let $R$ be a commutative ring spectrum of finite nonnegative telescopic
height at a prime $p$. We prove that the continuous algebraic
$K$-theory of the category of localizing motives over $R$ has
telescopic height $\operatorname{ht}_p(R)+2$. The upper bound follows from
the purity theorem for $K$-theory applied twice. For the lower bound we find one ring
at each height whose motives are detected by an iterated trace, and
move this nonvanishing to every ring of the same height. This uses two
properties of continuous $K$-theory of motives as a function of the
coefficients: it only sees the coefficients near the relevant heights,
and its Bousfield class does not change under finite faithfully
etale extension. The second property is proved with multiplicative
norms on equivariant localizing motives.
