## Abstract

Let $E_n$ be the Morava theory of the Honda formal group over
$\mathbf{F}_{p^n}$. We prove that $E_n^{hG}\to E_n$ is
$\mathbb{E}_\infty$-descendable for every closed subgroup $G$ of the extended
Morava stabilizer group, both in $K(n)$-local spectra and in
ordinary spectra. In particular, this holds for
$L_{K(n)}\mathbb{S}\to E_n$. For groups of finite $p$-cohomological
dimension, the algebra retraction comes from a section after
relative $p$-finite completion of a finite join. Finite cyclic
quotients are treated by a unital arity obstruction tower,
whose possible obstructions are restricted to the arities
$p,p^2,\ldots,p^n$ by chromatic vanishing for partition spectra.
