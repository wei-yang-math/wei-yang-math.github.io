## Abstract

Let $A$ be a perfectoid ring and let $R$ be a smooth $A$-algebra.
We prove that the $p$-completed algebraic $K$-theory of $R$ agrees
with its $p$-completed homotopy $K$-theory. If $R/pR$ has relative
dimension at most $d$ over $A/pA$, we also prove that the fiber of
$K(R;\mathbf{F}_p)\to K(R[1/p];\mathbf{F}_p)$ has no homotopy groups
above degree $d$. This answers Question 5.14 of Antieau--Mathew--Morrow.
The proof constructs a perfectoid coefficient extension which induces
injections on the homotopy groups of $K$-theory modulo $p$ with support
on $p$ and receives a map from a mixed-characteristic rank-one
perfectoid valuation ring.
