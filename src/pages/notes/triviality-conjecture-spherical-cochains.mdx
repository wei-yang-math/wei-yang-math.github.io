## Abstract

We prove the triviality conjecture of Heuts and Land: for $n\geq1$
and an $\mathbb{E}_{n+2}$-ring spectrum $A$, the augmented cochain algebra
$C^*(S^n;A)$ is $\mathbb{E}_{n+1}$-trivial over $A$ if and only if $A$ is
rational. The proof constructs, at each prime $p$, a $D_p$-structure
on the original multiplication of $A_{(p)}$. A comparison between
configuration spaces and representation spheres turns the operadic
obstruction into a functional on cochains. Equivariant normalization
and one additional little-disks direction give the required product
and unit compatibilities. Hahn's chromatic-acyclicity theorem and the
Hopkins-Smith nilpotence theorem then imply rationality.
