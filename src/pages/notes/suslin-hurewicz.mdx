## Abstract

For an infinite field $F$ and an integer $n\geq 1$, Suslin constructed a natural
homomorphism $h_n:K_n(F)\to K_n^M(F)$ from Quillen $K$-theory to Milnor
$K$-theory whose composite with the canonical product map
$K_n^M(F)\to K_n(F)$ is multiplication by $\pm(n-1)!$.
We prove Suslin's conjecture that the image of $h_n$ equals
$(n-1)!K_n^M(F)$ for every $n\geq 1$ and every infinite field $F$.
On the $m$th effective cover of algebraic $K$-theory for
$m=\lfloor n/2\rfloor+1$, $\mathbb{P}^1$-stabilization turns the $n$th
Chern class into a stable operation $c_n^{\mathrm{st}}$ over the prime field
satisfying explicit Bott-shift recurrences, while Adams operations show that
cover classes surject onto $K_n(F)/q$ away from the characteristic.
Away from the prime $2$ in characteristic zero, decomposing mod-$q$ cover
classes along the Bott tower into transfers of products of cyclotomic Bott
elements and Milnor symbols determines the image of $c_n^{\mathrm{st}}$.
At the prime $2$ in characteristic zero, the Bott-shift recurrences compare
$c_n^{\mathrm{st}}$ with $\pm((n-1)!/2^r)\theta_r$ up to a $2$-torsion
operation whose vanishing reduces to odd $n$ over $\mathbb{R}$, after which
we evaluate $\theta_r$ over $F(\sqrt{-1})$ and descend to $F$.
