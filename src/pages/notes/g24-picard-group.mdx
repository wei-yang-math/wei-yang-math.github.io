## Abstract

Let $E$ be the height-two Morava $E$-theory associated to the supersingular
elliptic curve over $\mathbb{F}_4$ at the prime $2$. We compute the Picard
group of $K(2)$-local $E^{hG_{24}}$-modules. Suspension generates a cyclic
group of order $192$. The proof obtains an Artin-Schreier $d_{23}$ in the
Picard descent spectral sequence from the $C_4$ calculation. Two finite
Postnikov intervals determine the transfer needed for this differential.
The Galois action on the remaining fifth filtration then excludes classes
outside the suspension subgroup.
